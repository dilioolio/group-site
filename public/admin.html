How this admin works

Creating & editing a hike

  • You must fill in title, date/time, meetup location, and a description of at least 20 characters. Save is blocked until all of those are set.
  • Date / time pickers default to today's month/year and 09:00 — only the day is left blank for you to pick. Time is in 5-minute increments.
  • Two active hikes can't share the same start time. Cancelled hikes don't block the slot.

Photos on a hike

  • For a new hike, save the basic details first — the photo uploader unlocks after that.
  • Photos upload instantly when you pick them. Save is disabled while an upload is in progress.
  • After upload, click any photo to make it the primary one (shown on event cards and the hero image). The primary has a green border and a ★.
  • Save is blocked if photos exist but none is primary.

Cancelling a hike

  • Each active hike has a red Cancel button on the dashboard.
  • You pick a reason (5 common options + Other up to 30 characters). The reason goes to attendees as part of the cancellation email and push.
  • Cancelled hikes show a big diagonal CANCELLED banner across their image everywhere they appear.

Material changes (date / location / status)

  • When you edit any of those three on an existing hike and click Save, attendees are automatically emailed + push-notified about the change. (Title and description edits don't notify.)
  • To skip the auto-notify on a particular save, tick Suppress change notifications at the bottom of the Details tab.

How notifications reach attendees

  • Everyone who RSVPs always gets the email — that's automatic and not opt-out-able from the app.
  • Push notifications (desktop / mobile) are opt-in. After saving an RSVP the user sees a "Get instant change alerts?" popup. Only those who click Yes (and Allow in their browser's own prompt) will receive pushes. This applies to cancellations, Notify, Broadcasts, and material edits.
  • Notify and Broadcast messages also appear as bubbles on the event page itself — even members who missed the email or push see the latest updates when they revisit the hike. Notify bubbles have a green border; Broadcast bubbles have a persimmon border and show the subject. Newest first.
  • The bubbles are only displayed on today's hike and upcoming hikes. They're hidden once the hike has passed.

Tabs inside the editor

  • RSVPs — see who's coming, plus an Invite-by-email box that sends a one-click "view & RSVP" email. Recipients still RSVP themselves.
  • Notify — send a quick free-text update to attendees. Goes by email and push. No confirmation tracking.
  • Broadcasts — for important messages. Each recipient gets a unique Confirm link. You see who's confirmed and who hasn't.

Public visibility rules (what members see)

  • The home page shows hikes from the last 90 days through any future date.
  • Cancelled and past hikes can't be RSVP'd to. Past hikes show a "This hike has already happened" banner; the description collapses with a See more toggle.
  • The "Open your private photo gallery" link only appears on the day-of and for 30 days after.

Member photo gallery

  • A shared per-hike album so attendees can post and view photos. Private to the people who RSVP'd for that specific hike.
  • Anyone with gallery access can download individual photos or every photo as a zip.
  • Members get a gallery link in their RSVP confirmation email; they can request a resend if lost.
  • Member-uploaded photos auto-delete 30 days after the hike date.
  • You can moderate (delete) any member photo from the gallery view.
